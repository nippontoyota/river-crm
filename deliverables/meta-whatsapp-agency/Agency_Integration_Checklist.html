Developer verification notes

Meta leads: verify leads_retrieval, pages_manage_metadata for Page subscriptions, and supporting Page/advertising permissions required by the chosen workflow. Configure the Page’s leadgen subscription and verify lead retrieval. Final permissions depend on the implementation and asset ownership.

Direct WhatsApp: validate whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management; add other permissions only where required. Configure the message webhook and subscribe the app to the intended WABA. Tokens remain revocable even without a scheduled expiry.

Credential handover: use an agreed password manager or secure secret-sharing method. We store credentials on the CRM server. Share only account IDs, access status and contact details in the returned checklist.
